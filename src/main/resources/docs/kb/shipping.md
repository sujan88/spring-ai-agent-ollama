# Shipping

## Shipping options

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 15 dollars. Orders over 50 dollars ship free with Standard shipping.

## Processing time

Orders placed before 2 pm Eastern ship the same business day. Orders placed on Saturday or Sunday ship on Monday.

## Tracking

A tracking number is emailed when the order ships. Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear.

## Lost or late packages

If tracking shows no movement for 7 days, the package is treated as lost. Support can reship the order or issue a full refund once a lost package claim is filed.

## International shipping

We ship to Canada and Mexico only. Customs duties are paid by the customer on delivery.

## Address changes

The shipping address can be changed only while the order status is PROCESSING. After the order ships, the address cannot be changed.
